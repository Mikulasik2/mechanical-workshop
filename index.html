03About the game

A puzzle you solve with machinery

Mechanical Workshop is a physics-based puzzle game where you solve mechanical challenges by placing and connecting machine parts.

Each level hands you a goal and a tray of parts. Mesh gears, power them with a motor, route a rope over pulleys, let a weight fall, launch a crate off a spring and hit the switch that lights the room. If it fails, adjust and try again.

The campaign spans 40 levels in three chapters, from a single gear to multi-stage machines that run a whole factory. Between levels, the Workshop sandbox lets you build whatever you can imagine.

There's no single right answer. Just mechanical experimentation.

What's in the workshop

  • 40 Puzzle Levels

    A hand-crafted campaign in three chapters, from your first gear to the Final Assembly.

  • Physics-Based Building

    Build working mechanical systems and watch them react — torque, gravity and momentum included.

    Experiment freely outside campaign restrictions, with every part available and unlimited.

06How it works

Three steps. One button.

  1. 01Build

    Place mechanical parts from your tray onto the workshop wall.

  2. 02Connect

    Link parts with ropes and belts to create your machine.

  3. 03Play

07Workshop mode

Your own sandbox

Outside the campaign, the Workshop gives you a large empty wall and every part in unlimited supply. There are no goals and no limits. The Workshop is part of the full Android game.

  • Build without campaign restrictions
  • Experiment with different mechanisms
  • Save custom machines
  • Load them later
  • Explore built-in demo machines
