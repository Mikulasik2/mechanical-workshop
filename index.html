Stills

What to record, and why

  • L30 The Great Workshop is the hero. It has all five stations, from POWER to FINAL ACTIVATION, with gears, belts, two ropes, pulleys, weights, a fan, the ball, dominoes, the crate, a spring, a switch, the LINE DRIVE and the generator. The room stays dark until the end, then the lamps light one by one. No other level has that payoff.
  • L20 Master Contraption II gives the hook close-up. Its power section has the biggest gears and a visible belt, close together, and they turn from the first frame.
  • Workshop → Demo Machines → Crazy Machine, opened with EDIT COPY, not PLAY DEMO. That way the ∞ tray and the SAVE button are on screen, which reads as "your own sandbox".
  • Backups: L28 Factory Control (the room lights up system by system) and L21 Double Drive (one motor, a drawbridge, a single strong lamp).

Strongest moments

  1. L30 lamps: the dark hall lighting up lamp by lamp. This is the emotional peak, so show it in real time.
  2. Gear snap: a dragged gear clicks into mesh with a brass flash. It shows the main idea in one second.
  3. Crate launch: the spring throws the crate up to the CRATE LIFT switch, and the LINE DRIVE starts.
  4. 9 kg gate lift: two weights fall on ropes and the gate rises slowly and heavily.
  5. MACHINE COMPLETE with all three stars ticking in.

Audio

  • All sound in both cuts is the game's own. It was recorded with the picture: motor start, gear layer, belt, pulley, rope creak, impacts, spring, switch click, lamp buzz and level complete. The end card uses the game's own lamp_click, lamp_on and room tone. The hook adds the game's motor_start at 55 %.
  • In the 2× sections the sound is sped up with a time-stretch, so pitch stays natural.
  • No music is used. If you add some, look for a minimal, slow industrial pulse around 90–100 BPM. Use a muted kick and low synth bass, with metallic percussion that leaves room for the gear clicks. Bring in one warm pad when the lamps turn on. Use only a track you own or have licensed.

Production notes

  • On screen, the gear, pulley, weight, belt and rope are placed live with real mouse input. The other 11 parts of the 16-part machine were already in place when recording started. The run is the game's own known 3-star solution, and the result it shows (12.3 s, 3 stars) is genuine.
  • The soft ring that shows where the finger is was drawn by the recording script. It is not part of the game UI. Mobile trailers normally show a touch marker like this.
  • The run is shown at 2×. It is honest editing, but if store rules or your own preference call for it, add a small "2× SPEED" tag in the corner. Do not slow the lamp finale down.
  • Fonts: Inter Display (OFL) for the overlays. Colours come from scripts/visual/palette.gd: BRASS_LIGHT, LAMP_ON and STEEL_DARKEST.
  • Google Play rules: if this goes on the store listing, store pages usually crop or play the video at 16:9. The end card's "ANDROID" text is not the official Google Play badge. Use the official badge only under Google's badge guidelines.
